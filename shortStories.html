Morden day Icarus 

    As I wired the last copper cable ready for lift off
    My tongue can't taste the air, but these tungsten wings defy the sun
    Is this what nature's bounty was made for?
    petroleum exhausting out the engine as I soar higher and higher
    Despite the weight of these man-made wings, my past is heavier
    Up towards the sky I leave my home, to newer heights
    Faster, faster, faster not realizing how dangerous it is to be alone
    Maybe the extraterrestrial can welcome me to their community
    But soon I realize that I have overshot my destination
    The isolation and darkness answers my call
    As the wings of science defy nature's laws
    Is there a punishment for breaking our mothers heart?
