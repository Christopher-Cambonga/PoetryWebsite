Falling 

    I feel like I'm falling, with the wind under my body
    it's sound gradually piercing my ears forcing my face upwards
    cooling every part it interacts with.
    Mind you I cannot see as my descent increases exponentially,
    shutting my eyes to what will come next.
    I'm falling everything is sitting perfectly still like an
    audience at the climax of a play, frozen... I'm dealing with no
    hesitation the air surrounds me enveloping me like a witch's spell.
    I'm falling, wouldn't anyone be willing to break it? No then oh well.
    I'm falling, my body against the sunlight with its blue sky
    or maybe the moonlight with a starry night.
    I have fallen, when will I hit the ground?

Closed Doors 

        Tiles that align, dirt divided from the grass,
        Bricks that don't fit perfectly, windows with tinted glass,
        metal bars that burn in the summer, cameras that watch the mass,
        an access control vestibule,that could barely fit your ass,
        moreover, an obstacle in one's path is tough to grasp.
        Monty halls? probabilty of success? mathematical tasks...
        so paradoxical these golden oppurtunties masks,
        knock? ring the bell? ignore the stranger? 
        once you've walked up, prepare to run away, because alas!
        Never turn a knob, if you are unaware of the slob
        Never peak through the hole, if the key was never in your soul.
        Failure is not an option, but a reality that grows legs.
        Encompassing your shadow, as the light blinds the journey.
        Take courage to walk any path, but beware of open doors
        because as you get closer... SLAM! dreams may stay dreams.
