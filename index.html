A Secret Promise 

    Shadows move when you least expect them to,
    if light could reflect all the damage done,
    maybe you'd see that life is all not blue,
    that running takes more than the rays of sun,
    looking ahead tricks your other senses
    Like the smell of roses I cannot pick
    as if the thorns were blind to our lenses
    despite all beauty everything becomes sick
    the roots had never been truly cared for  
    but even if my hand bleeds with each prick
    Broken were my thoughts for you that were born
    Whether it's the right or my left hand out
    it was always your decision to shout.
